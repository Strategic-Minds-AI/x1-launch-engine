## High-quality prompt engineering workflow (operational, not a promised performance multiplier)
1. SOURCE-GROUNDED BRIEF: retrieve approved assets, audience, trade-specific offer, business facts and citations before generating claims.
2. ROLE SEPARATION: have market researcher, conversion strategist, visual director, accessible frontend engineer and independent critic produce separate artifacts.
3. CONSTRAINT-FIRST SPEC: lock design tokens and sections; distinguish must-have, forbidden claims, data contracts and conversion objective.
4. TASK DECOMPOSITION: research -> offer -> objections -> message hierarchy -> components -> content -> tests. Build one trade canary, then expand.
5. FEW-SHOT EXEMPLARS: provide 3 credible comparable pages as patterns, never copy protected source design or textual content.
6. STRUCTURED OUTPUT: each trade SitePack has trade, user intent, evidence, hero, 3 services, CTA, form schema, SEO, proof requirements, validation.
7. EXPLICIT RUBRIC: judge legibility, authentic imagery, content specificity, mobile responsiveness, interaction, claims evidence, privacy and speed.
8. CRITIQUE-REVISE: bounded three cycles of independent screenshot critique and targeted repair with visual diff evidence.
9. DETERMINISTIC QA: test every category, preview, wizard step, valid/invalid form, keyboard access, noindex header, broken links, and mobile/desktop.
10. GOVERNED PUBLISH: require verified contractor identity, service footprint, authentic reviews, photos/rights, price policy, CRM delivery, analytics and approval.
No prompt technique guarantees particular rankings, conversion rates or design valuation.
