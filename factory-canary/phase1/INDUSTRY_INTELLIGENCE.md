## Search / content constraints

Current Google Search Central guidance requires people-first, useful, reliable content and warns against extensive automated publishing whose primary purpose is manipulating rankings. Automated or AI-assisted content still requires accuracy, relevance, quality, factual review and appropriate context about how it was produced.

Therefore the WLE scale model must:
- generate genuinely useful trade/market content rather than city-name swaps;
- preserve verified business facts only;
- avoid fabricated local offices, reviews, pricing, licensing, demand and ranking guarantees;
- keep automated content under human/independent review before indexable release;
- use noindex on preview fleets;
- make authorship/process/source evidence available where appropriate;
- validate metadata, structured data, page experience, accessibility and conversion flows.
