# 08 IMAGE MANIFEST
## Present
Illustrative external images are loaded from images.unsplash.com and tagged as inspiration, NOT completed floor installations. Asset URLs and role-specific alternatives are in `lib/template.ts` and the static HTML/CSS. License and redistribution for client reuse have not been verified.
## Generation roles
- Hero: 16:9, luxury automotive garage with physically accurate coated concrete surface.
- Residential service: 4:5 true garage/floor surface detail.
- Commercial service: 4:5 legitimate industrial/workspace coating.
- Decorative service: 4:5 architectural concrete material close-up.
- Gallery: 3:2 original finish visuals with honest substrate and spec.
- Social: 1:1 abstract material signature.
The image-brief generator exports role, aspect ratio, prompts, realism requirements and provenance requirements. It does NOT render images. Before client use require generation/provenance, human visual inspection, usage rights, alt text, content claim review, and asset hash bound to the template manifest.
